Main Level Highlights

The first floor features a spacious primary bedroom suite (13'7" x 16'6") with walk-in closet and private full bath, a generous living room (15'9" x 12'9"), an updated kitchen (15'3" x 13'1") with stainless steel appliances and cherry cabinetry, a formal dining room (15'9" x 13'1"), an additional bedroom (9'11" x 13'1"), a third bedroom (12'3" x 9'3"), and a second full bath — all connected by a long central hallway. The 3-car heated garage (43'4" x 30'7") provides exceptional vehicle and project space.

Finished Basement Highlights

The basement adds 1,178 sq ft of finished living area including an expansive recreation room (33'9" x 26'2") perfect for entertaining, a bedroom (11'7" x 22'3"), a versatile office/play room (12'6" x 13'1"), a full bathroom, and a dedicated utility/laundry area (17'11" x 26'2"). LVP flooring runs throughout the entire basement, complemented by recessed lighting and drop ceilings to create a bright, move-in ready lower level.

The SP/LP ratios for our core comparables range from 98.6% to 101.8%, indicating buyers are competing for well-priced homes. Properties with standout features — like your finished basement and oversized garage — are especially well-positioned to attract motivated buyers quickly.

Why This Range

The adjusted comparable values range from $665,000 to $740,000. Our recommended list price of $650,000–$675,000 positions your property competitively, accounting for:

  • The property's well/septic vs. public utilities on comps
  • A 2006 build vs. newer construction (2020–2024)
  • Private/gravel road access vs. paved public roads
  • Proximity to I-95 — closer than comparables, potential noise factor
  • Basement finish may not be fully permitted (common adjustment)
  • Strategic pricing to generate multiple offers and maximize net proceeds
