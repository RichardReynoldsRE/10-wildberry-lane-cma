Location Factor
The I-95 Adjustment, Quantified

Why a 6% discount, not 3% or 10%

10 Wildberry Lane sits close to the Maine Turnpike (I-95) corridor. Published research on highway-proximity impact on single-family values consistently shows a 3–8% negative adjustment for homes within 500–1,000 feet of an interstate, with the magnitude driven by tree buffers, topography, and the prevailing home value band.

The most apples-to-apples observation in our own data set is 436 Flag Pond Road: same town, same ~2-acre lot, same 3-car garage, same 4-bed profile, sold January 2026 (extremely recent), and it too is close to I-95. It closed at $617,000 — 4.3% under list. By contrast, interior-Saco comps (67 Mast Hill, 11 Cori) routinely closed at or above list.

A 6% adjustment splits the academic range and is the number the Flag Pond comp validates empirically.
