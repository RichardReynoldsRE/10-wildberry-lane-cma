Why two reports?

The original April 2 CMA correctly identified the bones of the pricing conversation but used a narrower comp pool. The April 19 update widens the net, incorporates the seller’s May 2024 purchase price, references appreciation pro-rated to each comp’s close date, and applies a location adjustment for Wildberry Lane’s proximity to I-95. The net effect: a sharper $685,000 recommendation that sits just under the $700K filter threshold and reflects what the Maine Listings record actually shows.
